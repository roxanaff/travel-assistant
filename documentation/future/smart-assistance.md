# Future Direction — Smart Assistance

- Activity suggestions based on preferences, trip type, and itinerary gaps.
- Museum, restaurant, nightlife, and event discovery.
- Automatically sourced opening hours, entry prices, closed days, and booking requirements.
- Map-provider imports, booking links, and ticket purchasing.
- Travel-time and route suggestions.
- Weather-aware packing suggestions. When a trip is within the available forecast range, show a per-day forecast in
  the itinerary and refresh it automatically as the forecast changes.
- Notifications for reservations, departure times, and budget limits.
- Exportable itinerary and expense reports.
- A conversational assistant once reliable trip, activity, availability, preference, and expense data exists.
