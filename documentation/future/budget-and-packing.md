# Future Direction — Budget and Packing

## Budget and expenses

- Expected dates on planned costs, with close-date reminders or visual indicators.
- Per-day and per-traveller summaries.
- Planned-cost grouping and sorting alternatives.
- Collapsed budget and expense summary cards.
- Savings progress toward the trip target budget. Money set aside would be recorded separately from planned costs and
  expenses, with progress and the remaining amount needed to reach the target shown clearly.
- Negative expenses and refunds.
- Receipt capture, upload, and OCR.
- Multiple currencies and exchange-rate conversion. Each expense would retain the paid amount and transaction currency,
  while its converted trip-currency amount would be used in totals. The paid amount could be shown prominently with the
  converted trip amount in a quieter parenthetical treatment.

  The conversion method remains open: an exchange rate may be entered manually for each expense, or an external rate may
  be looked up when a foreign-currency expense is created. An automatic option would need a documented source, rate
  date, caching behaviour, and an editable saved rate so historic totals do not change unexpectedly.

- Shared payments, splits, and settlements.

## Packing

- Adding default items after the initial empty-state choice, with duplicate prevention based on normalised names.
- Custom categories and free-text category names when Other is selected.
- User-customisable default templates.
- Rule-based suggestions based on trip type, activities, destination, duration, and laundry access.
- Weather-aware suggestions.
- Live movement of surrounding rows while a packing item is dragged.
- Improve touch drag-and-drop so an item can be dropped into its intended position without needing to move it sideways to
  avoid the original drop target.
- Shared and personal packing lists for group trips.
