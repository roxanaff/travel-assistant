# Future Direction — Accounts and Collaboration

## Account hardening

- Password reset by a single-use email link with an expiry.
- A transactional email provider and sending address for account recovery.
- Rate limits for registration, sign-in, and account-recovery requests.
- Device/session management, including a list of active sessions and controls to revoke a device or sign out everywhere.

## Shared trips

- Invitations, collaborators, and shared-trip indicators.
- Roles and advanced section permissions.
- Shared itinerary and expenses.
- Payment tracking, automatic split calculations, and settlement summaries.
